Privacy notice

This notice explains how Mopani Studio handles information from Garden Site Reading enquiries and visits.

Effective 13 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Mopani Studio is a registered business name of Jana Opperman, a sole trader (ABN 45 377 510 285), and is a residential planting-design and urban-greening practice based in Ellenbrook, Western Australia. Privacy questions, access requests, correction or deletion requests, and complaints may be sent to hello@mopanistudio.com.

2. Information we collect

For the Ellenbrook pilot, the website prepares an email draft using your name, suburb, garden type and any garden question you choose to add. The website does not transmit or store your form entries. When you send the draft, the studio receives the information in its inbox and uses your email address to reply about the pilot. Please leave out street addresses and photos at this stage. If we arrange a visit, we will request any further information directly.

Depending on the request, Mopani Studio may collect:

  • your name, email address and optional telephone number;
  • the suburb, postcode and address of the property;
  • your relationship to the property and authority to make decisions about it;
  • property type, approximate size and stage of occupation or construction;
  • your stated garden concerns, goals and preferred outcome;
  • recent photographs, plans or other files you choose to provide;
  • access, attendance and safety information relevant to a possible visit; and
  • correspondence, review decisions, appointment and service records.

Please do not upload identification documents, financial records, medical information or photographs of people. Remove unrelated personal details from plans and documents where possible.

3. Why the information is collected

The information is used to:

  • assess whether a Garden Site Reading falls within Mopani Studio’s location, scope, safety and capacity boundaries;
  • identify missing evidence without asking you to repeat information;
  • communicate an accept, information-required, defer, waitlist or decline/referral decision;
  • arrange and perform an accepted Site Reading;
  • prepare the Garden Conditions & Planting Opportunity Report;
  • provide later design services when separately agreed; and
  • maintain reasonable business, legal, payment and service records.

Request information is not used for unrelated marketing unless you separately choose to receive it.

The workflow does not automatically accept or decline a project. A person reviews the information before Mopani Studio communicates a suitability decision.

4. Systems used

Mopani Studio uses a small set of service providers to operate the request pathway:

  • Zite, operated by Restly, Inc. doing business as Fillout, for standard service requests and related file collection when that pathway opens;
  • Notion for the internal request and client operating record;
  • Google Workspace, including Gmail and Google Drive, for studio email and controlled document or evidence storage; and
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting.

These providers may process or store information outside Australia and may use subprocessors. Their processing locations and subprocessors can change. Mopani Studio applies access restrictions to its business accounts and uses the information only for the purposes described in this notice.

5. Photographs and property files

Photographs and plans are requested only where they help Mopani Studio understand the property, identify service boundaries and avoid an unnecessary or unsuitable visit. They are not published, used in a portfolio or shared for promotional purposes without separate permission.

If a request does not become a client engagement, Mopani Studio reviews the closed request and deletes submitted photographs, plans and supporting files within 90 days after closure. A request is closed when it is declined, withdrawn, or no longer under active review. Evidence may be retained longer only where reasonably necessary to meet a legal requirement, address an active dispute, or respond to a request from the person who supplied it.

6. Accepted clients and retention

When a request becomes a pilot visit or a paid service, relevant information becomes part of the project record. Project, transaction and design records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the service, maintain professional and financial records, respond to later questions and meet applicable legal, tax or insurance requirements. Information that is no longer required is deleted or de-identified where practical.

7. Access, correction, deletion and complaints

You may ask what personal information Mopani Studio holds about you, request a correction, or ask for information to be deleted. Send the request from the email address used to contact the studio so identity can be checked without collecting unnecessary additional identification.

If essential assessment information is withdrawn before a decision or visit, Mopani Studio may be unable to continue the request. Some business records may need to be retained despite a deletion request where retention is reasonably required or legally authorised.

If you have a concern about how your information has been handled, email hello@mopanistudio.com with the subject Privacy complaint and enough detail for the issue to be understood. Mopani Studio will review the concern and respond within a reasonable period. If the Privacy Act 1988 applies and you remain dissatisfied after raising the matter with Mopani Studio, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Sharing and disclosure

Mopani Studio does not sell submitted personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers above, with a professional adviser needed to operate the business, where you direct or consent to the sharing, or where disclosure is required or authorised by law.

A referral decision normally identifies a category of professional. Your photographs, plans and contact details are not sent to that professional unless you separately authorise it.

9. Security and incidents

Mopani Studio uses controlled business accounts, access restrictions and data-minimisation practices intended to reduce unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No internet transmission or cloud service can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you believe information has been sent to the wrong place, accessed improperly or lost, contact hello@mopanistudio.com promptly.

10. Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when the request process, service providers or handling practices materially change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. This notice describes Mopani Studio’s operating standard and does not reduce any privacy rights available under applicable law.
