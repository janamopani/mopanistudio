Free one-hour garden consultations for Ellenbrook residents

Dear Ellenbrook neighbours,

I’m Jana, a graduate landscape designer and the founder of Mopani Studio. My ambition is to make Perth the greenest city on the planet by 2050. I’m starting in my hometown, right here in Ellenbrook.

I want our children to grow up with more shade on the streets they walk and more life in the gardens around them. The choices we make at home now will help shape the Whadjuk (Perth) and Binjareb (Peel) they grow into.

That’s why I’m inviting Ellenbrook residents to help test the Garden Site Reading, my on-site garden consultation. I’ll walk through your outdoor space with you, see how you use it, look at where sun and shade fall, and work out where planting could make a difference. If you take part, you’ll receive a free digital report with practical next steps once I’ve finalised the pilot method.

I’m looking for a mix of homes, from bare blocks and small courtyards to established gardens. By seeing what’s possible in different spaces and hearing what people have the time, money and confidence to care for, I can build a service that helps more residents get started. You can take part even if you’ve never gardened and have only one corner you’d like to change.
