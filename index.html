How to replicate this in Google Sites

Note: While AI assistants cannot directly construct pages inside your private Google account, you can mirror this layout on sites.google.com within minutes following the guide below!
1

Create New Site & Choose Simple Theme

Go to sites.google.com, start a Blank site, and under the Themes tab select "Simple" or "Aristotle" with neutral font styles (e.g., Modern or Classic).

2

Setup Pages Hierarchy

Under the Pages tab on the right panel, add 4 pages: Home, Design Process, Blog, and Contact.

3

Add Sections & Collapsible Group (Process)

For the Design Process section, drag in Collapsible Group widgets from the Insert panel to replicate the accordion steps (Discovery, Wireframing, Refinement, Launch).

4

Embed HTML Component (Optional)

You can embed custom interactive widgets or this entire clean page into Google Sites by clicking Insert > Embed > Embed Code and pasting code!
