What the studio does

A planting-led landscape practice.

The service begins with evidence from the property—not a shopping list or a free quote. Each later stage is separate, considered and entered only when it is appropriate for the site and the client.

01 · Entry service

Garden Site Reading

A 60-minute on-property reading of sun path, exposure, existing vegetation and simple soil-hydration conditions, followed by a printed and digital report.

02 · By invitation

Planting Design

A site-specific planting system developed after the property has been read: placement, species logic, quantities and establishment requirements before plants are purchased.

03 · Approved plans

Planting Establishment

Guided nursery selection, softscape installation and practical education. Client presence is part of the method; physical planting is optional and limited to suitable low-risk activity.

Read before design

The governed client journey.

Observation comes first. The plan is approved before the nursery. Plants are selected before they are established. At every stage, the client knows what is being decided and why.

The studio observes sun path, exposure, existing vegetation and simple soil-hydration conditions. This is site orientation—not laboratory soil testing, arboricultural assessment, engineering advice or a planting-design workshop.

The Garden Conditions & Planting Opportunity Report records seasonal conditions, areas of opportunity and an annotated planting-opportunity plan. It is designed to remain useful even when the client does not commission a planting design.

A Site Reading does not automatically become a design project. Suitable clients who want to proceed may be invited into a separate design-direction, proposal and agreement process before design work begins.

Placement, species logic, quantities and establishment requirements are resolved and approved before the nursery visit. The nursery is not where the garden is designed from whatever happens to be on the shelf.

The client joins the studio to select stock from the approved plan and learn how size, growth habit and visible root condition affect the choice. Participation is educational and decision-based.

Mopani Studio installs and establishes the approved planting plan on an installation-ready site. Client presence is part of the learning method. Physical participation is optional and limited to low-risk activity approved by the studio.

The agreed handover explains the immediate establishment requirements. Any later review or support is defined and priced separately before it is promised.

Softscape boundary

Mopani Studio does not provide mowing, weeding, routine maintenance, demolition, structural landscaping, retaining walls, paving, decking, fencing, major earthworks, irrigation alteration, electrical or drainage work, pesticide or herbicide application, tree removal, substantial pruning, work at height, or specialist arboricultural, engineering or laboratory-soil advice.

About Mopani Studio

A landscape practice for the living part of the property.

Mopani Studio is a residential softscape urban-greening design and planting-establishment studio based near Ellenbrook, Western Australia.

The practice exists to help residents understand the conditions already acting upon their garden, then make deliberate planting decisions that can be maintained in real life. Education is not an add-on: the client learns how the property, planting system and selected species work together.

The studio’s method is independently aligned with the practical direction of urban-greening policy. This describes a shared field of work; it does not imply government appointment, accreditation, partnership or endorsement.
