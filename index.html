Our Methodology

The Client Journey

We build strong relationships from day one. You are involved in every stage—from hand-selecting stock at the nursery to engaging younger family members in sustainable gardening.

We visit your property to evaluate soil structure, sun exposure, drainage, and existing wind corridors. We analyze how your site fits into Perth’s broader Mediterranean climate zone and formulate a preliminary strategy for micro-greening.

You join us directly at local plant nurseries. Together, we inspect root systems, discuss plant choices, and build your customized ‘Planting & Placement Plan’. We explain why specific species suit your micro-climate and plan for future expansion.

On planting day, we work alongside you to remediate sandy or nutrient-poor soils, establish root zones, and plant correctly. This hands-on step is ideal for families with young children to pass down fundamental urban greening principles.

To combat plant mortality across Perth’s summer heatwaves, we perform quarterly site audits throughout the first year. Future check-ups and maintenance advice can be booked seamlessly via our online client portal.
