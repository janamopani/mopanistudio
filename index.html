We’re looking for a mix of gardens, from newly built blocks and small courtyards to established yards. You can take part with the space, budget and experience you have today. We’ll start by walking through the garden with you, following the routes you use most.

Why this pilot starts at home

Roads and roofs can be built quickly. Shade, healthy soil and habitat take seasons. The choices we make around our homes today will help determine how much greenery is there for the people living on Whadjuk (Perth) and Binjareb (Peel) Country in 2050.

Read different gardens

A bare block, a small courtyard and a yard with mature trees call for different decisions. Your garden helps us learn which observations matter and how to make the advice useful.

Make one change you can care for

Your reading begins with where you walk, where you need shade and what already grows. We look for a step that fits your space, time and budget.

Grow beyond the park

Public parks and street trees matter. So do the places between them. Keeping existing trees and making room for living cover at home can add shade and habitat where people spend their days.

A garden begins with the way you move through it.

Mopani is an urban greening studio founded by Jana Opperman in Ellenbrook. We start with your everyday route through the garden and work out where planting can bring shade, habitat or comfort to the places you use.

Read, design and establish

A Garden Site Reading helps you understand your site and choose where to begin. Planting design and hands-on establishment are separate services, discussed when they suit the garden and your goals.

Urban greening resources

Mopani Studio independently supports the goals of Western Australia’s Urban Greening Strategy. Working with Mopani starts with the greenery you already have and the places where more shade or habitat could fit. We help you choose planting you can establish and keep alive.

What the Let’s Grow Action Plan does

  • Growing MoreSupport planting and tree retention on public and private land, and improve how canopy is measured.
  • Growing TogetherSupport community participation, partnerships with Noongar people and Aboriginal-led greening.
  • Growing StrongerStrengthen local greening plans, plan for resilient planting and water, and connect green spaces.

WA’s commitments include planting one million trees by 2035 and reaching 30 per cent tree canopy cover in Perth by 2040.
