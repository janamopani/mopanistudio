2050 Vision

Give Perth’s gardens time to grow.

Roads and roofs can be built quickly. Shade, healthy soil and habitat take seasons. Mopani Studio’s vision for 2050 is a Perth and Peel where more households know how to grow and care for the greenery their neighbourhoods need.

Make room for greenery

As neighbourhoods grow, space for trees and smaller plantings needs to be considered alongside buildings, paths and services.

Put shade where people use it

Start with the places you walk, pause or sit. Look at where shade falls in the afternoon and how that changes with the season.

Plant for the water we have

South-west Australia’s April–October rainfall has declined by about 16% since 1970. Choose plants that suit the site and plan how you’ll water them while they establish.

Grow habitat at home

Keep useful existing vegetation where it suits the site. Add layers of planting and aim for flowers across different seasons.

Start while there’s time

WA aims to plant one million trees by 2035 and reach 30% tree canopy cover in Perth by 2040. What we plant and keep alive today has time to mature.

Take one useful step

Start with one exposed path, one bare bed or one place you already enjoy. Choose a change your household can care for through its first summer.

From the garden journal

Before You Buy Plants: Read the Site

A planting list is not yet a planting system.

Before species are chosen, the property needs to be understood as a set of conditions: where light moves, where exposure is strongest, what vegetation is already present, how moisture behaves and how the resident needs to use the space.

Observation creates the brief

The Garden Site Reading does not pretend to be a complete design session. It creates the evidence and orientation needed to decide whether self-directed planting, further investigation or a designed planting system is the responsible next step.

Heat, Exposure and Seasonal Water

The same plant can meet a different reality from one part of a property to another.

A useful garden record separates broad regional climate from the local conditions created by walls, paving, shade, wind, existing roots and patterns of watering. Those differences influence placement and establishment.

Read first; prescribe later

Mopani Studio records visible conditions and simple hydration observations. It does not replace laboratory soil testing, irrigation advice or specialist diagnosis where those are genuinely required.

Why the Plan Comes Before the Nursery

The nursery is a place of selection, not the place where the garden should first be designed.

An approved planting system gives each purchase a reason: position, mature role, quantity and relationship to the rest of the garden. It also gives the client a standard against which available stock can be assessed.

Participation with structure

The client joins the nursery session to understand and select from the approved plan. Their involvement builds practical knowledge without turning the design process into an improvised shopping trip.

Why Urban Greening Has to Begin Before 2050

The greenery Perth and Peel will need in 2050 has to begin establishing before then.

Homes, roads and hard surfaces can be added faster than trees can spread canopy, roots can establish and habitat can return. This makes time one of urban greening’s most important resources.

The built city grows quickly. Trees, roots and habitat need time. Perth and Peel have 24 growing years before 2050—and the choice to use them begins now.

The six-part 2050 story explains how population growth, heat, water and human health make urban greening a practical priority now—not a project to leave until conditions become harder.
